Alzaib Karovalia

Robotics integration engineer working on autonomous systems and robot learning.

I studied Mechatronic Systems Engineering at Simon Fraser University in British Columbia (2016 to 2021), and I am now doing a master's in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Waterloo. Alongside my studies, I work as a Systems Designer at OTTO Motors by Rockwell Automation, where I bring autonomous mobile robot fleets into service in 24/7 automotive and industrial plants: integrating them with customer PLCs and facility systems, building simulation and monitoring tools, and tracking down failures across robot, software and PLC interfaces.

Outside work, I build projects that go from embedded firmware to vision-based driving to vision-language-action policies in MuJoCo, and write up what worked and what did not.
