Alzaib Karovalia

Robotics integration engineer working on autonomous systems and robot learning.

I hold a BASc in Mechatronic Systems Engineering from Simon Fraser University in British Columbia (2016 to 2021), and I am now completing a master's in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Waterloo. Alongside my studies, I work as a Systems Designer at OTTO Motors by Rockwell Automation, where I bring fleets of autonomous mobile robots into service in 24/7 automotive and industrial plants. My work covers integrating the robots with customer PLCs and facility systems, building simulation and monitoring tools, and diagnosing failures across the robot, software and PLC interfaces. Before OTTO, I worked on embedded control at Shearwater Research and on control systems at the National Research Council Canada.

Outside work, I build robot-learning projects such as training a vision-language-action policy for a simulated robot arm in MuJoCo, teaching it with reinforcement learning, and earlier, self-driving systems and embedded firmware. Each project has a full write-up below.
